Your next adventure starts in a terminal.

Download a client, start or join a server, and invite up to three friends. Pick Warrior, Rogue, Mage or Cleric and descend into Emberfall.

This is the project website. Playing requires the terminal client and a running game server. No public game server is configured here.

Pick your class

Four classes, each with four skills that unlock as you level. Mix them in a party.

Warrior

Tough front-liner. Most HP and armor, keeps monsters on himself.

  • CleaveLv 1
  • War CryLv 3
  • Iron SkinLv 5
  • BerserkLv 8

Rogue

Deadly and slippery. 20% crit, 12% dodge, huge burst damage.

  • BackstabLv 1
  • Smoke BombLv 3
  • Fan of KnivesLv 5

Mage

Glass cannon. Weak in melee, devastating spells from range.

  • FireballLv 1

Why you'll keep playing

An endless dungeon that gets deadlier — and richer — the further you go.

Weapons, armor and trinkets with rolled stats: crit, lifesteal, dodge, gold find and more.

Goblin King, Bone Lord, Wraith Queen, Demon Prince… and the Ancient Dragon at depth 8.

Go down instead of dying when friends are near — they have 20 seconds to revive you. Shared XP.

Gold Rush, Blood Moon, Loot Frenzy and Treasure Goblins that run away with piles of loot.

◇Bounties & titles

Three bounties always on the board, 29 achievements and titles to show off on the leaderboard.

ΩSix biomes & a story

From the Old Cellars to the Throne of the Abyss. Find all 12 lore scrolls to learn what woke up below.

AEndgame

Defeat the Abyss Lord, upgrade gear to +10 at the blacksmith, and be reborn stronger with every rebirth.

Parties of four with bonus XP, personal boss loot, pings, party chat and travel straight to your friends.

⚡Tiny & fast

A single ~7 MB binary. True-color terminal UI that only redraws what changed — smooth even over SSH.

Start a dungeon with friends

Download, extract, launch. No Git or Go required.

Download server ZIP ↓

  1. Install Node.js LTS once.
  2. Extract the server ZIP into a folder you want to keep.
  3. Windows: double-click start-server.cmd.
    macOS / Linux: run sh start-server.sh in that folder.

The first launch installs dependencies automatically. Keep the server window open. Open http://localhost:3143 for one-command client launchers. Press Ctrl+C to stop.

Your world and heroes stay in the extracted folder. Reuse it on your next launch and back up your saves before updating. Same-network friends can use the address printed in the server window; Internet play needs a reachable host or TCP port forwarding.

Launch your downloaded client and choose Register. On the same computer, use localhost:3143. Friends use your server’s reachable address. Press P to form a party.

On Windows, run the downloaded .exe from Windows Terminal. On macOS / Linux, use the command above. A terminal of at least 100 × 30 is recommended.
