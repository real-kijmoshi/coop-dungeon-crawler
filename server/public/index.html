A co-op dungeon crawler for your terminal. Grab friends, pick a class, go deep.

Play now — one command, nothing to set up
$ curl -fsSL __BASE__/play | sh
Install the cdc command
$ curl -fsSL __BASE__/install | sh
Just watch — live, right in your terminal
$ curl __BASE__/watch

The launcher downloads a ~7 MB client once, caches it, and connects you to this server.

PS> irm __BASE__/play.ps1 | iex
Install the cdc command
PS> irm __BASE__/install.ps1 | iex
Just watch — live, in Windows Terminal
PS> curl.exe __BASE__/watch

Works best in Windows Terminal. The client is a single .exe with no installer.

Pick your class

Four classes, each with four skills that unlock as you level. Mix them in a party.

Warrior

Tough front-liner. Most HP and armor, keeps monsters on himself.

  • CleaveLv 1
  • War CryLv 3
  • Iron SkinLv 5
  • BerserkLv 8

Rogue

Deadly and slippery. 20% crit, 12% dodge, huge burst damage.

  • BackstabLv 1
  • Smoke BombLv 3
  • Fan of KnivesLv 5
  • Shadow StrikeLv 8

Mage

Glass cannon. Weak in melee, devastating spells from range.

  • FireballLv 1
  • Frost NovaLv 3
  • Chain LightningLv 5
  • MeteorLv 8

Cleric

Healer and undead-slayer. Keeps the whole party standing.

  • HealLv 1
  • SmiteLv 3
  • SanctuaryLv 5
  • Holy NovaLv 8

Why you'll keep playing

An endless dungeon that gets deadlier — and richer — the further you go.

/Random loot

Weapons, armor and trinkets with rolled stats: crit, lifesteal, dodge, gold find and more.

CommonMagicRareEpicLegendary

DBosses & lairs

Goblin King, Bone Lord, Wraith Queen, Demon Prince… and the Ancient Dragon at depth 8.

%Real co-op

Go down instead of dying when friends are near — they have 20 seconds to revive you. Shared XP.

★World events

Gold Rush, Blood Moon, Loot Frenzy and Treasure Goblins that run away with piles of loot.

◇Bounties & titles

Three bounties always on the board, 22 achievements and titles to show off on the leaderboard.

⚡Tiny & fast

A single ~7 MB binary. True-color terminal UI that only redraws what changed — smooth even over SSH.

Manual download

Prefer to grab the binary yourself? Each is a single file, no installer.

Windows x64 Windows ARM64 macOS Apple Silicon macOS Intel Linux x64 Linux ARM64

Then run it with this server:

chmod +x cdc-*            # macOS / Linux
./cdc-linux-amd64 -server __BASE__
